Version 1.7.1 refines the sidebar, PDF page thumbnails and the PDF tool palette; no new data, permissions or purchases. Labels below are Korean (English); the app follows the device language.

New in this version
- Sidebar: folder controls at the row's end; + on the selected folder adds a window. PDF thumbnails keep each page's shape. Palette: undo/redo grouped, larger colours, line-style widths.

From 1.7.0
- Video PiP: a downloaded video, or a web/YouTube video in the in-app browser, can keep playing in a small floating window inside the app ("PiP로 보기"; also automatic when leaving the tab, Settings can turn it off). It is an in-app window, not system PiP; no background audio. Drag, resize, close or return to the tab.
- AI panel: a third layout, 떠 있는 사이드바 (Overlay sidebar), floats over the document instead of pushing it.
- PDF: adding, deleting or moving pages appends an incremental update and keeps the original bytes; pulling past the last page shows a label before it adds a page.

From 1.5.0
- AI panel (AI button, left rail). In a PDF, 노트에 넣기 (Add to note) and 짚어주기 (Point It Out).
- 찾아보기 (Look Up): asked once ("켜기" Turn On / "나중에" Later; Settings > AI > 찾아보기). Lassoed or selected text is looked up on Wikipedia's search API from the device (a term and the heading above; no user or device data). For passages, formulas and figures, two optional AI steps via our server (signed in, provider agreed to, within the AI allowance): AI explains (text up to 400 characters, heading, document title, a small picture for a formula or figure) and AI picks the matching article (passage up to 300 characters plus up to four Wikipedia results to OpenAI's Decisions API, which returns only probabilities and one title).

Plans and AI Credit
- Every purchase is on one screen, 요금제 (Plans): Settings (gear) > 구독·사용량 (Plan & usage) > 요금제 보기 (View plans). Also the profile menu, or a second folder on Free.
- Current plan, Free/Plus/Pro at App Store prices, AI 충전 (AI Credit), Restore, manage subscription, Terms, Privacy Policy, auto-renewal terms.
- AI Credit adds to the account's AI allowance and never expires. Our server keeps the balance, so it needs sign-in. Then AI 충전 > the price button: the card says "충전했어요" (Credit added) and the balance goes up. Not restorable; it stays on the account.

From earlier versions
- "AI로 찾기" (Find with AI) for a school not in the directory needs sign-in and sends only the school name, country and app language; our server searches via Anthropic's API and returns the school's own links for review.
- Friend invites (profile menu > 친구 초대): both accounts get in-app AI credit when an invited friend creates a first folder (at most 5 friends); no cash value, not transferable, no purchase needed. bandal.io/r/CODE links open the app (Associated Domains).
- AI goes through our server to Anthropic or OpenAI after a per-provider consent sheet (5.1.2(i)); the server stores no questions, materials or answers.
- Sync is optional (own iCloud or Google Drive). Recordings stay on the device. In-app browser (17+). Delete Account: Settings > 계정 > 계정 삭제.

How to test
- Sign in (Account button, left rail; Apple or Google) for AI, AI Credit and invites. In a PDF, tap the AI button and ask; circle printed text with the lasso for Look Up.

External services
- Bandal server (auth.bandal.io / Supabase): account (ID, email, name, nickname); AI usage records; App Store transaction status; invite codes and who invited whom (account IDs); the school directory.
- Anthropic, OpenAI: AI requests after consent. Apple: Sign in with Apple, In-App Purchase. Google: Sign in with Google, Google Drive API for sync.
- Wikipedia (Wikimedia): Look Up. The 찾아보기 button can also preview the browser's search engine (no cookies or history).
- No analytics, advertising or crash reporting. No device identifiers are collected.

Not distributed in the EU or China mainland.
