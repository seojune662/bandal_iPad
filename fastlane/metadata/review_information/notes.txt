Hello,

Version 1.4.0 changes how AI works: users no longer enter their own API keys. AI now goes through our server (a relay on Supabase Edge Functions) to Anthropic or OpenAI, metered by a monthly allowance per plan. It also adds Bandal Pro (two auto-renewable subscriptions in the existing "Bandal Membership" group) and AI Credit (a consumable).

How to try AI
- Sign in (Account button in the left rail: Sign in with Apple or Google). AI needs an account because the allowance is per account; everything else works without one.
- Open a course, then the AI panel (the "AI" handle on the right edge of the workspace), type a question and send. Before the first request to each provider a consent sheet (guideline 5.1.2(i)) names the company, what is sent and links its privacy policy; nothing is sent if the user cancels. Consent can be withdrawn in Settings > AI.
- The model button under the input ("Auto") lets the user pick Auto, an Anthropic or an OpenAI model, and the thinking depth.

What our server does with AI requests
- It checks the account's plan and remaining allowance, forwards the request to the provider with our API key, streams the answer back and records token counts and cost. It does not store questions, materials or answers.

Plans (all optional; nothing already made is ever locked)
- Free: 1 course and 10 materials on this iPad; signed in, a small AI allowance (about 30 short questions a month).
- Bandal Plus (monthly / yearly, existing): unlimited courses and materials, AI about 400 short questions a month.
- Bandal Pro (monthly / yearly, new, higher level in the same group): Plus with 4x the AI allowance. During a free trial the AI allowance is Plus's; the paywall says so.
- AI Credit (consumable): adds to the current allowance, never expires, not restorable (stated on the purchase sheet).
- Where to see them: Settings > 구독 (Subscription) > 반달 멤버십 보기 / AI 충전. When the allowance runs out, the app opens the paywall (free: Plus and Pro; Plus: Pro) or the credit sheet (Pro).
- Restore Purchases is on the paywall and in Settings > 구독. Terms of Use (Apple standard EULA) and Privacy Policy are linked on the paywall.
- Purchases are verified on our server from the signed transaction (App Store Server Library) only to set the AI allowance; course and material limits are judged on the device.

Other features (unchanged)
- Sync (optional, free): Settings > 동기화, the user's own iCloud or Google Drive. We run no server for documents.
- Lecture recording stays on the device; in-app browser (age rating 17+).
- Account deletion: Settings > 계정 > 계정 삭제.

External services
- Bandal server (auth.bandal.io / Supabase): account (ID, email, name, nickname); AI usage records (token counts, cost, model); App Store transaction status for the AI allowance.
- Anthropic, OpenAI: AI requests, only after the per-provider consent.
- Apple: Sign in with Apple, In-App Purchase. Google: Sign in with Google, Google Drive API for sync.
- No analytics, advertising or crash reporting.

Regional differences: not distributed in the EU or China mainland.
