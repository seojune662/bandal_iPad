Hello,

Version 1.4.1 adds three things. No new in-app purchases; purchases, AI, sync and account deletion work as in 1.4.0. The app starts in the device language (Korean or English); labels below are Korean (English).

1. First-run setup (4 steps, once, on a new install)
- "나중에" (Later) leaves it on steps 1-3; step 4 ends with "시작하기" (Get started).
- Language: Korean or English, applied at once; later in Settings > 일반 (General) > 언어 (Language).
- School: search our school directory and pick one; the school's websites (portal, LMS, library...) appear as shortcuts in the sidebar. No sign-in needed.
- Invite code: an optional 6-character code from a friend; "없어요, 건너뛰기" (I don't have one, skip). The system Paste button reads the clipboard only when tapped.

2. School setup with AI
- When a school is not in the directory, "AI로 찾기" (Find with AI) asks our server to find its official websites. The server runs a web search through Anthropic's API and returns only links on the school's own domain. The user reviews, edits or removes them before saving; they are labelled "AI가 찾은 정보 · 검토 전" (Found by AI · Not reviewed yet).
- It needs sign-in (3 searches per account per day) and sends only the school name and country the user typed, plus the app language: no personal data, notes or documents. We pay for it; the user's AI allowance is not used.
- Links the user edits before saving are also sent to our server (no personal data) as a suggestion for the shared directory.
- Without sign-in the user can enter the school name alone: "직접 입력" (Enter it myself).

3. Friend invites
- Profile menu (left rail) > 친구 초대 (Invite a friend), or Settings > 계정 (Account) > 친구 초대: the account's invite code with Copy and Share.
- When an invited friend signs in and creates their first course, both accounts receive AI credit worth about 100 AI uses. It is the same in-app AI credit as before: used after the monthly allowance, never expires. An account is rewarded for at most 5 friends.
- The reward is AI usage inside the app only. It has no cash value, cannot be transferred, exchanged or withdrawn, and unlocks no paid plan. No purchase is needed to invite or to be invited.
- The share text and "링크 복사" (Copy link) carry the link https://bandal.io/r/CODE. On an iPad with the app installed the link opens the app with the code filled in; the user still taps "이 코드 적용" (Apply this code). The app adds Associated Domains (applinks:bandal.io) for this.

How to test
- New install: the setup appears on first launch. In step 2, with the country chip on United States, type "Stanford" and pick Stanford University (AI-found, so its links are shown for review); tap "이 학교로 설정" (Use this school). Or type any name and choose "직접 입력".
- AI search and invites need an account: Account button in the left rail (Sign in with Apple or Google).

Unchanged from 1.4.0
- AI goes through our server to Anthropic or OpenAI after a per-provider consent sheet (5.1.2(i)); the server stores no questions, materials or answers.
- Plans: Free, Bandal Plus, Bandal Pro and AI Credit (consumable) in Settings > 구독·사용량 (Plan & usage). Restore Purchases is on the plan screen.
- Sync is optional (the user's own iCloud or Google Drive). Recordings stay on the device. In-app browser (17+). Account deletion: Settings > 계정 > 계정 삭제 (Delete Account).

External services
- Bandal server (auth.bandal.io / Supabase): account (ID, email, name, nickname); AI usage records; App Store transaction status; the invite code and which account invited which (account IDs only); the school directory.
- Anthropic, OpenAI: AI requests after consent. Anthropic also runs the school website search (school name, country and app language only). Optional "AI 설명" (AI explanation; off by default, Plus/Pro): a selected passage (up to 400 characters, its heading, the document title) goes once through our server to the cheapest model of a provider already agreed to, within the AI allowance; it never opens a purchase screen.
- Apple: Sign in with Apple, In-App Purchase. Google: Sign in with Google, Google Drive API for sync.
- Wikipedia (Wikimedia): "찾아보기" (Look Up). Automatic Look Up is asked once ("켜기" Turn On / "나중에" Later; off in Settings > AI > 찾아보기). Then printed text circled with the lasso in a PDF, or text selected in a note, goes from the device straight to Wikipedia's search API (the text or a key term from it, and the heading above; a generic app identifier, no user or device data, not through our server). The "찾아보기" button on selected text also opens the browser's search engine in a small preview without cookies or history.
- No analytics, advertising or crash reporting. No device identifiers are collected.

Not distributed in the EU or China mainland.
