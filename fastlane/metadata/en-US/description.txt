Bandal is an iPad app that brings everything you need for university study onto one screen. Organize PDFs, notes and handwriting by course, and split the screen to view several materials side by side.

PDF and handwriting
- Open lecture PDFs and write on them with a pen, ballpoint or highlighter. Apple Pencil pressure and tilt are supported.
- Select ink with the lasso to move it, and add shapes and text boxes.
- Highlight and annotate the source text, rearrange or delete pages, and undo.
- Create blank handwriting notes with your choice of paper, and whiteboards.

Notes
- Write Markdown notes with headings, lists, checklists, tables and formulas.
- Place PDF pages and notes side by side and link them.

Workspace
- Drag tabs to split the screen horizontally or vertically. Courses and your reading position are restored the next time you open the app.
- A built-in browser for school sites and course materials, to-dos and schedules, and Apple Calendar integration.

AI
- Sign in to ask questions with your materials attached and make summaries, quizzes and flashcards. No API key needed.
- Auto is the default; you can also pick an Anthropic or OpenAI model and how deeply it thinks. Every model draws on the same monthly allowance.
- The app asks for consent before the first request to each provider. Questions and attachments go through the Bandal server to the provider you chose; the server records only how much AI you used and does not store the content.

Storage
- Everything is stored on your iPad. No account is needed.
- You can sync with your other devices through your own iCloud or Google Drive, for free. Your materials are never uploaded to Bandal's servers.

Bandal Membership (auto-renewable subscriptions)
- Free: on this iPad, create 1 course and 10 materials; signed in, a little AI each month (about 30 Auto questions).
- Bandal Plus: unlimited courses and materials, and about 400 Auto questions' worth of AI a month.
- Bandal Pro: everything in Plus with 4× the AI (about 1,700 Auto questions' worth).
- AI Credit (one-time purchase) adds to this month's allowance and never expires.
- New subscribers get a 1-week free trial before the first charge. Payment is charged to your Apple Account. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel it in Settings › [your name] › Subscriptions.
- When a subscription ends, the courses and materials you made stay open and editable.
